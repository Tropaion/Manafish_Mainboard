## SamacSys symbols and footprints

These were generated by SamacSys (Component Search Engine) and are used under
SamacSys's terms of use:

- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/symbols/BMI270.kicad_sym`
- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/symbols/SHT45-AD1F-R2.kicad_sym`
- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/symbols/ABM8-272-T3.kicad_sym`
- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/symbols/V6K100DUHM3_H.kicad_sym`
- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/symbols/FDMC8327L.kicad_sym`
- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/footprints/BMI270.pretty/BMI270.kicad_mod`
- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/footprints/SHT45.pretty/SON80P150X150X59-5N.kicad_mod`
- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/footprints/ABM8-272-T3.pretty/ABM8272T3.kicad_mod`
- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/footprints/V6K100DUHM3.pretty/V6K100DUHM3H.kicad_mod`
- `hardware/mainboard/custom_components/footprints/FDMC86139P.pretty/FDMC86139P.kicad_mod`

Some of them were modified for this board:

- `V6K100DUHM3H.kicad_mod` and `V6K100DUHM3_H.kicad_sym`: the two exposed
  cathode pads (pads 9-12) carry the number of the cathode pins they belong to
  (8 and 5), and the hidden symbol pins 9-12 were removed. Before, the pads had
  no net and touched the cathode pins.
- `FDMC86139P.kicad_mod`: the exposed drain pad is numbered 5, so it joins the
  drain pins; the origin was moved to the centre of the package; the 3D model
  path points to `custom_components/3d/`. The `FDMC8327L` symbol's default
  footprint now names this library.
- `ABM8272T3.kicad_mod`: the courtyard was trimmed from 5.6 x 4.8 mm to
  4.1 x 3.3 mm (the pads plus 0.25 mm), so the crystal fits next to the RP2354A.
- `ABM8272T3.kicad_mod`, `BMI270.kicad_mod` and
  `SON80P150X150X59-5N.kicad_mod`: the 3D model path points to
  `custom_components/3d/`.

  12 thermal vias are drilled 0.3 mm instead of 0.2 mm, to meet this board's

  6ef68694) from KiCad's package data. KiCad's library has no model for this
